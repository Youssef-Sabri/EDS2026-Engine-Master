Governing model equations

1 · Displacement
V_d = 4(πB²/4)·S ≈ 0.001598 m³
2 · Air mass flow
ṁ_air ≈ ηᵥ · ρ_m · V_d · N / 120
3 · Brake power
P_b = T_b · (2πN / 60)
4 · Mean effective pressure
BMEP = 4πT_b / V_d ;  IMEP = BMEP / η_m
5 · Slider-crank kinematics
x(θ) = r·((1 − cosθ) + (1/λ)·(1 − √(1 − λ²sin²θ))),  λ = r/L
6 · Ideal-gas charge density
ρ = P / (R·T),  R_air = 287.05 J/(kg·K)
7 · Wiebe mass fraction burned
x_b(θ) = 1 − exp[ −a·((θ − θ₀)/Δθ)^(m+1) ]
